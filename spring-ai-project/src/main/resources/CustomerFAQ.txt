CUSTOMER FAQ
Sample Banking FAQ for RAG / Semantic Search Testing

NOTE:
This is a fictional FAQ created for software development and RAG demonstrations.


Q1. What is the standard daily transaction limit?

A1. The standard maximum daily transaction limit for an eligible retail customer is INR 100,000.


Q2. What is the international transaction limit?

A2. The sample international transaction daily limit is INR 500,000.


Q3. When does an international transaction require additional authorization?

A3. An international transaction above INR 250,000 requires additional authorization in the sample transaction policy.


Q4. What is the minimum monthly income for a personal loan?

A4. The sample Loan Policy specifies a minimum monthly income of INR 30,000.


Q5. What is the personal loan amount range?

A5. The sample personal loan amount ranges from INR 50,000 to INR 2,000,000, subject to eligibility and approval.


Q6. What is the sample personal loan interest rate?

A6. The sample Loan Policy uses an interest rate of 10.5% per annum for RAG testing.


Q7. What documents are required to open a savings account?

A7. Customers need valid identity proof, address proof, and other documents required during the verification process.


Q8. What is the minimum monthly balance for the sample savings account?

A8. The sample Account Opening Policy specifies a minimum monthly balance of INR 5,000.


Q9. What account types are supported in the sample policy?

A9. The sample account types are Savings Account, Current Account, Salary Account, and Basic Digital Account.


Q10. What transaction statuses are supported?

A10. Supported sample transaction statuses are INITIATED, PENDING, AUTHORIZED, COMPLETED, FAILED, REJECTED, and REVERSED.


Q11. Can a failed transaction be considered completed?

A11. No. A failed transaction must not be considered successfully completed.


Q12. What should a customer provide when contacting support about a transaction?

A12. The customer should provide the transaction reference number.
