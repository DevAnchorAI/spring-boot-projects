Account Opening Policy

Document Type: Sample Banking Account Opening Guide

Version: 1.0

Note: This fictional document is intended for RAG and semantic-search testing.

1. Eligibility

Customers must provide valid identification and address information and complete the required customer verification process before an account can be opened.

2. Required Documents

  • Government-issued identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Recent photograph where applicable
  • Tax identification information where applicable
  • Additional documents requested during verification

3. Account Types

Sample account types include:

  • Savings Account
  • Current Account
  • Salary Account
  • Basic Digital Account

4. Savings Account

A standard savings account can be opened after successful identity verification and completion of the account-opening application.

5. Minimum Balance

For this sample policy, the standard savings account requires a minimum monthly balance of INR 5,000.

6. Digital Account Opening

Eligible customers may start the account-opening process through a digital application. The application remains subject to identity verification and applicable approval checks.

7. Account Activation

An account becomes active after all mandatory verification and account-opening steps are successfully completed.

8. Important Facts for RAG Testing

  • Standard savings account minimum monthly balance: INR 5,000
  • Identity verification is required before account activation.
  • Sample account types: Savings, Current, Salary, and Basic Digital.

9. Disclaimer

This is a fictional sample document for software development, RAG, and semantic-search demonstrations. It is not an actual banking policy.
